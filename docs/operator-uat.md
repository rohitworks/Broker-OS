# Broker OS pilot UAT

Before launch, verify one complete PID-to-RID journey: create/activate a property, upload/approve media, open its private page, create/activate a requirement, generate matches, approve a shortlist, and confirm the ledger entry.

Then mark the PID RENTED or SOLD. Confirm it no longer matches, its page is unavailable, its ledger recipients receive one closure action, and retries do not duplicate it. Review the failure queue, auth access, opt-out behavior, and a restore from an encrypted backup before enabling real clients.

Daily operator review: pending verification, active requirements, failed messages, failed closure actions, opted-out contacts, and stale inventory.
